# Design System

## Mood

- calm
- editorial
- adult education
- grounded
- warm
- understated
- scholarly

## Avoid

- SaaS blue
- glassmorphism
- glowing gradients
- excessive pills
- oversized radius
- floating dashboards
- decorative Islamic clichés
- arch
- crescent
- star
- mosque dome
- fake manuscript

## Color Direction

```text
Background   #F6F5EF
Surface      #FCFBF7
Ink          #20231F
Primary      #214E3B
Secondary    #688574
Accent       #C59B5C
Border       #DDDCD4
Muted        #777A72
```

## Typography

- Heading: editorial serif
- Body/UI: readable sans
- LMS lebih dominan sans

## Radius

- controls: 6–8px
- cards: 10–14px
- featured panel: max 16px

Tidak semua section harus berbentuk card.
